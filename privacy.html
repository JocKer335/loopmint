Privacy notice

What happens when you contact LoopMint, request a trial, or use this website.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who you are contacting

LoopMint LLC operates LoopMint. Its registered address is in Albuquerque, New Mexico, United States. WhatsApp is our main route for trial requests, plan questions and support. You can also contact us at inquiries@officialgnf.com. For sales and setup, message +44 7597 648884. For technical support after activation, message +44 7907 504571.

Details you provide

When you request a trial or ask about a plan, you may give us your name, country, WhatsApp number, device, chosen plan and message. The website prepares a WhatsApp message on your device; you choose whether to send it. We also receive the details you send in later WhatsApp or email conversations. This website does not ask for card details in its trial form or store full card numbers.

How we use your details

We use these details to answer your request, check the viewing and setup options for your country and device, provide your trial or paid service, confirm an order, and resolve support or refund questions. We use order records when needed to handle payments and our record-keeping obligations.

WhatsApp and other services

If you choose to send a message through WhatsApp, WhatsApp and its parent company Meta process that communication under their own terms and privacy information. Our website is published through GitHub Pages. Some pages load Google Fonts. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them.

Optional Meta tracking

With your choice, Meta Pixel measures visits to selected pages and WhatsApp contact actions so we can understand our advertising. It does not run before you accept optional tracking. You can reject it and still use the website and request a trial. We store your tracking choice in your browser so we can remember it. The site also stores a display theme preference and a short-lived setting for a one-time popup.

Change your optional tracking choice at any time. Rejecting after accepting stops future tracking when the page reloads; it does not remove information already received by Meta.

How long we keep information

We keep customer conversations while they are needed to manage an active plan, answer support questions, investigate a problem or process a refund. Some transaction records may need to be kept longer for accounting or other legal obligations. You can ask us to review or delete your information by emailing inquiries@officialgnf.com.

Your choices and questions

Contact us at the email above if you want to ask what information we hold, correct it, request deletion, or object to a use of it. Where UK or EU privacy law applies, you may also have additional rights and a right to contact your local data protection authority. We will review requests in light of the rules that apply.

For a trial, only share the details needed to help us respond. Never send full card details or passwords in a website form or WhatsApp message.
