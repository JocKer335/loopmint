FAQs

The questions customers ask most.

Open the accordion for quick policy answers and support terms.

Ask Sales & Setup
What do I need to watch?

A supported device and a stable broadband connection. The finder covers smart TVs, sticks, boxes, phones, tablets, and laptops.

How long does setup take?

Many setups take only a few minutes once the device details are confirmed. Timing can vary by device, app, and support availability.

Is there a contract?

Choose an upfront term of 1 month, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, or 3 years. Confirm the renewal terms before paying.

Will it keep buffering?

Playback depends on your internet connection, device, app, and the selected source. We help you choose suitable settings and troubleshoot common buffering issues.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. You can request a free 24-hour trial before choosing a paid plan, and no payment card is needed to submit the request.

Is LoopMint available in my country?

Availability can vary by location, content, app, and device. Tell Sales & Setup your country and device before choosing a plan, and ask them to confirm the options available to you during the trial.
