BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

Trial and plan terms

A plain-language guide to the offer, payment and renewal process.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

The business and how to reach us

LoopMint LLC operates LoopMint. Its registered address is in Albuquerque, New Mexico, United States. For trials, plans, payment questions and device setup, contact Sales & Setup on WhatsApp at +44 7597 648884. You can also email inquiries@officialgnf.com. For technical issues with an active plan, contact Technical Support at +44 7907 504571.

Free 24-hour trial

You can request a free 24-hour trial before choosing a paid plan. No card details are needed to submit the trial request. We confirm the available setup route and the trial start with you on WhatsApp. The trial ends after its 24-hour period; it does not start a paid plan or charge you automatically.

Paid plans and payment

The homepage shows the available plan durations and prices. Before you pay, our team confirms the total price, currency, plan length, start and end dates, supported devices and payment route with you on WhatsApp, and provides the full registered address in the order details. The website itself does not take a card payment or store full card numbers. Do not pay until you have received and accepted the final details.

Manual renewal

Plans do not renew automatically. At the end of your paid period, you can choose whether to contact LoopMint and buy another term. We do not charge for a new term without your request and payment confirmation.

Availability and performance

Viewing options can differ by country, device, app and plan, and can change over time. If a particular channel, match, film or series matters to you, ask us to confirm its current availability and applicable terms for your location before paying. Picture quality depends on the chosen viewing option, your device and your internet connection. Use the trial to check your own setup.

Help and refunds

Message Technical Support if an active service has a playback or login problem. If you are unhappy with a paid plan, our 30-day refund policy explains how to make a request. Nothing on this page removes consumer rights that apply to you under local law.
