UK VIEWER GUIDE

How to test live TV in the UK before choosing a plan

Use a free 24-hour LoopMint trial on your own screen. Check the viewing options that matter to you, then compare the published plan prices and confirm the amount you would actually pay.

Request a free 24-hour trialVisit LoopMint home

Published by LoopMint · 30 September 2026

About this guide: LoopMint publishes this article about its own paid service. The prices below come from the LoopMint plans page as displayed on 30 September 2026. Trial requests are available worldwide; this guide answers questions for people watching in the UK.

Tell Sales & Setup that you will watch in the UK, even if your WhatsApp number has another country's dialling code. Your viewing location and device matter more than the country code on your phone number.

Before your 24 hours start

Give the team your exact television, stick, box, phone or computer model. Ask which player app to use, where to install it and whether the app has a separate fee. Confirm the trial start and end time in writing. You do not need payment card details to request the trial.

Some UK searches call this an IPTV trial. That label alone cannot confirm which viewing options, player apps or picture quality will be available on your screen. If a particular channel, match, film or series matters to you, ask the team to confirm its current availability and applicable viewing rights for the UK before paying.

Test your normal viewing setup

  1. Install the recommended player from its official app store or website. Check the device setup routes if you need help.
  2. Try the trial on the screen and home connection you will actually use. Check it again at the time of day you normally watch.
  3. Open the categories you care about. Test picture and sound, remote navigation, subtitles and the programme guide where available.
  4. Ask one real support question while the trial is active. Keep the answer and any offer details in writing.

The full 24-hour trial checklist gives you more ways to assess your own device and connection.

Choose only after you have the details

Ask for the plan length, number of simultaneous connections, start and expiry dates, support route and total charge in writing. LoopMint plans renew only when you ask. Read the plan terms and refund policy before paying. If you are outside the UK, use the worldwide availability guide for the same request process.

Message to send with your trial request

“I will watch in the UK on [exact device model]. Please confirm the player app and any separate fee, when my 24-hour trial starts and ends, the viewing options available here, and the final price and currency if I choose a plan.”
