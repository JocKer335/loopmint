UK VIEWER GUIDE

How to test live TV in the UK before choosing a plan

Use a free 24-hour LoopMint trial on your own screen. Check the viewing options that matter to you, then compare the published plan prices and confirm the amount you would actually pay.

Request a free 24-hour trialVisit LoopMint home

Published by LoopMint · 30 September 2026

A note about this guide: LoopMint publishes this page about its own service. The euro prices below were on our plans page on 30 September 2026; check that page for today’s offer. You can request a trial worldwide, and this guide is for people who plan to watch in the UK.

When you message us, say that you’ll watch in the UK. Your WhatsApp number might have a different country code; that’s fine. We need your viewing location and device to check the right setup.

Before your 24 hours start

Send the full model name of the TV, stick, box, phone or computer you want to use. Ask which player app to install, where to get it and whether it needs a separate activation. Get the trial’s start and end time in writing. You don’t need to enter card details to request one.

“IPTV trial” is a common search, but the label doesn’t tell you what will work on your screen. If you care about a particular channel, match, film or series, ask whether it’s currently available in the UK before you choose a paid plan.

Test your normal viewing setup

  1. Install the recommended player from its official store or website. Our device setup guides can help if you get stuck.
  2. Watch on the screen and home connection you’ll use day to day. Try again at the time you usually sit down to watch.
  3. Open the categories you care about. Check picture, sound, remote controls, subtitles and the programme guide.
  4. Ask support a real question while the trial is running. Save the answer and offer details so you can refer back to them.

The full 24-hour trial checklist gives you more ways to assess your own device and connection.

Choose only after you have the details

Before paying, ask for the plan length, how many screens can watch at once, start and expiry dates, support contact and total charge. LoopMint renews a plan only when you ask. Read the plan terms and refund policy first. Watching from somewhere else? Our worldwide guide explains what to include in your message.

Message to send with your trial request

“I will watch in the UK on [exact device model]. Please confirm the player app and any separate fee, when my 24-hour trial starts and ends, the viewing options available here, and the final price and currency if I choose a plan.”
