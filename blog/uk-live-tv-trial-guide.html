UK VIEWER GUIDE

How to test live TV in the UK before choosing a plan

Use the 24-hour trial on the screen you’ll watch in the UK. Then check the plan, included player activation and final payment currency before choosing.

Request a free 24-hour trialVisit LoopMint home

Published by LoopMint · 30 September 2026 · Updated 9 October 2026

About this UK guide: LoopMint sells the plans described here and accepts requests worldwide. The euro viewing prices below were checked on 9 October 2026; the main pricing page has the current offer.

When you request a trial, say where you’ll actually watch. A UK viewing address and a WhatsApp number with another country code are perfectly compatible details; the phone selector is there to format your number.

Before your 24 hours start

Send the full model of your TV, stick, box, phone or computer. Ask which player to install and whether its activation is separate from viewing access. Have the app ready, then get the trial’s start and end time confirmed so you can use the time properly.

No card details are needed to request the trial. If a channel, match, film or series is your reason for trying it, mention that now. “UK channels” is much less specific than naming the programme you want.

Test your normal viewing setup

  1. Install the confirmed player through its official store or developer route. The device setup centre can help you find the right instructions.
  2. Use your usual screen and home connection, rather than testing only on a phone beside the router.
  3. Try the viewing you care about, including sound, subtitles, guide information and remote controls.
  4. Try again during your normal evening viewing time. Keep a note of any error and the item being played.
  5. Ask support a specific question and save the reply with the offer details.

The full trial checklist gives you a way to organise those notes. If a must-have item is unavailable, ask about it before moving to a paid plan.

These are euro amounts, not fixed pound prices. Before paying, ask for the final amount and currency, then check any exchange or payment-provider fee. If you add a simultaneous screen, include that charge in the same quote.

For a compatible player, the six-month and one-year plans include one-year activation. Two-year and three-year plans include lifetime activation. The one-month plan includes no free activation. The player’s licence term doesn’t extend the viewing plan.

Choose only after you have the details

Keep the confirmed price, plan length, included activation, screen allowance and start and expiry dates together. LoopMint renews plans when you ask. Read the plan terms and refund policy before paying.

If you’ll watch outside the UK, use the worldwide request guide. A trial in one location doesn’t settle the setup or available viewing for another.

A message to start with

“I’ll watch in the UK on [device model]. Can you confirm the player, when the trial starts, and whether [programme or event] is available? If I choose a plan, please confirm the total and payment currency.” Send it to Sales & Setup.
