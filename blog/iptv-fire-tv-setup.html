FIRE TV SETUP

How to set up IPTV on Fire TV or Fire Stick

The exact player and sign-in method depend on your Fire TV model, app-store region and viewing service. Get those details before installing anything.

About this guide: LoopMint sells viewing plans. The setup steps below are for Fire TV; plan-specific details apply to LoopMint.

Before you start

Start with the exact model name. You can usually find it in Settings on your Fire TV. Check that the device is online and up to date, then tell your provider where you will be watching. Ask which player app to install, where to find its official listing and whether it needs a separate activation.

Keep your setup message nearby when you install the app. It may include a username and password, a playlist link or another private sign-in method. Don’t post those details in a public group or send a screenshot that shows them.

Install and sign in

  1. On the Fire TV home screen, open Find or Search and look up the app your provider named.
  2. Open its Amazon Appstore page. Check the developer and make sure it supports your device before installing.
  3. Open the app and choose the sign-in option your provider gave you.
  4. Enter the details carefully, then give the app a moment to load its categories and guide.
  5. Try a few options. Check the picture and sound, and see how easy the app is to use with your remote.

If you can’t find the search menu, Amazon’s Fire TV home-screen guide shows where it lives. The labels can vary a little between models and software versions.

If the player is missing or does not open

If the app doesn’t appear in the store or won’t open, pause before downloading a file from a random search result. Ask Sales & Setup to check your Fire TV model and store region and recommend a supported option. If the app opens but won’t sign in, double-check the login method and details, restart the app, then refresh its guide.

When you contact support, include your device model, the app name and the exact error message. Keep passwords and playlist links out of the message. For buffering or playback trouble, try the playback checklist first.

Understand activation and viewing access

Some player apps have their own licence. With LoopMint, compatible player activation lasts one year on six-month and one-year plans, and lifetime on two-year and three-year plans. The one-month plan doesn’t include player activation. Check that the app works on your exact device and remember: an app licence doesn’t extend the viewing plan.

Follow the Fire TV setup route, or try the setup during a free trial before choosing a plan.

Still stuck at a step?

Send Sales & Setup your country, Fire TV model and a short description of the problem. Leave out your password; the team can help you check the app and setup route.
