COUNTRY AVAILABILITY

Where is LoopMint available?

LoopMint accepts free 24-hour trial requests and paid plan requests worldwide. Tell our team where you will watch so they can confirm the viewing options and setup route for your location.

Request a free 24-hour trialVisit LoopMint home

Published by LoopMint · 30 September 2026

About this guide: LoopMint publishes this page about its own service. You can ask about a trial or plan from anywhere in the world; the viewing options and supported app still depend on your country and device.

You can contact us from any country. When you do, tell us where you’ll watch and what device you own. Your WhatsApp number’s country code may be different from your viewing location.

How do you request a trial from your country?

In the trial form, choose the country code for your WhatsApp number. Then add your viewing country in the message. Here are three examples:

  • United Kingdom: choose the UK code if that’s the number you’ll use, then write “I’ll be watching in the UK.” See our UK trial and price guide too.
  • Ireland: choose Ireland if that’s your WhatsApp number’s country code, then say “I’ll be watching in Ireland.”
  • United States: choose the US code if it matches your number, then say “I’ll be watching in the USA.”

Those are just examples; you can ask about a trial or plan from any country. The selector is there to format your WhatsApp number, not to limit where you can use LoopMint. If you have a different country’s number, select its code and tell us separately where you’ll be watching. You don’t need to enter card details to request the trial.

What should you tell us?

Tell Sales & Setup your viewing country and the exact device model, plus its operating system if you know it. For example, “Samsung TV, model [number]” is more useful than just “Smart TV.” Our device setup guide covers Fire TV, Android and Google TV, Apple, Windows, Smart TVs and TV boxes; the exact app steps can still vary by model and store.

Before the trial starts, ask when your 24 hours begin and end, what it includes and whether you’ll need a separate player app. If you’re checking a particular sport or kind of viewing, mention it now so the team can check what’s available in your country. Then use our trial checklist to test it on your own screen.

What can vary by country?

The options you can watch during a trial or paid plan may vary with your country, device and current offer. If you want a particular channel, match, film or series, ask us about that title directly. A category label or sports image on a website can’t confirm that it’s available where you live.

Player apps vary too: an app that appears in one country’s store may not be available for every model or region. Ask for its exact name and whether it needs a separate activation. Our app and viewing service guide explains what each payment covers.

How do you check the price?

Our pricing page shows plans in euros. Before you pay, ask Sales & Setup to confirm the final amount and currency, including any app or extra-screen costs. Get the plan length, connection limit and start and expiry dates in writing too.

LoopMint plans renew only when you ask. Read the plan terms and refund policy before choosing. Your payment provider may also apply its own exchange rate or fee, so check the final amount there.

A useful message to send

“I will watch in [your country] on [exact device model]. I would like to try LoopMint for 24 hours. Please confirm the setup app, any separate app cost, the viewing options available here and the total price if I choose a paid plan.”
