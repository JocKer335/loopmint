Where is LoopMint available?

LoopMint accepts free 24-hour trial requests and paid plan requests worldwide. Tell our team where you will watch so they can confirm the viewing options and setup route for your location.

Request a free 24-hour trialVisit LoopMint home

Published by LoopMint · 30 September 2026

About this guide: LoopMint publishes this page about its own service. Trials and paid plans can be requested worldwide. The exact content, player app and picture quality you can use still need to be checked for your location and device.

Start with your country and the screen you own. A phone number's dialling code may be different from the country where you watch, so include your viewing country in the message you send to Sales & Setup.

How do you request a trial from your country?

Choose the calling code for your WhatsApp number in the trial form, then tell Sales & Setup where you will actually watch. These examples show the process for three countries:

  • United Kingdom: select the United Kingdom if it matches your WhatsApp number, write “watching in the UK” in your message, and use the UK trial and price guide.
  • Ireland: select Ireland if it matches your WhatsApp number, and write “watching in Ireland” in your message.
  • United States: select the United States if it matches your WhatsApp number, and write “watching in the USA” in your message.

You can request a trial or plan from other countries too. The country selector supplies your WhatsApp dialling code; it does not tell us where you watch. If your number belongs to a different country, choose its code and state your viewing location separately. Requesting the trial does not require payment card details.

What should you tell us?

Send Sales & Setup your viewing country, exact device model and operating system if known. Tell them whether you use a Fire TV device, Android or Google TV, Apple device, Windows computer, Smart TV or television box. The device setup guide shows the available routes, but the app and installation steps can vary by model and app store.

Ask when the 24-hour trial starts and ends, whether a separate player app has a fee, and what is included in the trial. If you need a particular kind of viewing, describe it before activation so the team can answer for your country. Our trial checklist gives you a practical way to test the result on your own screen.

What can vary by country?

The viewing choices available during a trial or paid plan can depend on your country, device, app and the current offer. A sports image or a category label on a website does not confirm access to a named channel, match, film or series. Ask Sales & Setup to confirm current availability and applicable viewing rights for the place where you will watch.

App-store availability can differ between countries and device models. Confirm the recommended player's exact name and any separate licence fee before paying for an app. Our app versus viewing service guide explains the difference between those payments.

How do you check the price?

The LoopMint pricing page displays plan prices in euros. Before paying, ask the team to confirm the final amount and payment currency for your order, along with any separate app or additional screen charges. Confirm the plan length, simultaneous connection limit and start and expiry dates in writing.

LoopMint plans renew when you request renewal. Read the plan terms and refund policy before choosing a paid plan. If a payment method applies an exchange rate or fee, check its final amount with that payment provider.

A useful message to send

“I will watch in [your country] on [exact device model]. I would like to try LoopMint for 24 hours. Please confirm the setup app, any separate app cost, the viewing options available here and the total price if I choose a paid plan.”
