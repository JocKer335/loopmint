Where is LoopMint available?

You can request a LoopMint trial or plan from any country. Tell us where you’ll watch and which device you own so we can check the setup and current viewing options.

Published by LoopMint · 30 September 2026 · Updated 9 October 2026

About our availability: LoopMint publishes this guide about its own service and accepts requests worldwide. The supported app and available viewing still depend on your country and device.

You don’t need a UK, Irish or American number to contact us. The important distinction is between the country code of your WhatsApp number and the country where you’ll use the service. They may be different.

How do you request a trial from your country?

In the trial form, choose the code that belongs to your WhatsApp number. After the form opens your WhatsApp draft, add the country where you’ll actually watch before sending it.

For example, someone with a UK number who watches in Morocco would choose the UK phone code and add “I’ll be watching in Morocco.” Choosing a phone code formats the contact number; it doesn’t limit where you can request a plan or confirm your viewing location.

Our UK guide is for people watching in the UK. It’s one regional example, not a list of the only countries we serve. You can request the free 24-hour trial worldwide without entering card details.

What should you tell us?

Include your viewing country and full device model. If you know the operating system or already have a player, add that too. “Samsung [model number]” is easier to check than “Smart TV.” The setup centre covers the main device routes.

Tell us what you want to try, especially if a particular channel, match or series matters. Ask when the 24 hours begin and end and which player to install. Then follow the trial checklist using your own connection and screen.

What can vary by country?

App-store listings, supported models and available viewing can differ. A player available on one television in one store region may not be the right choice elsewhere. Ask for its exact name before paying for activation.

Check individual programmes as well as broad categories. A sports image or series count can’t establish whether your chosen item is available in your location. If you plan to travel, ask about that second location separately rather than assuming the original trial covers it.

The app and service guide explains why player compatibility and viewing access need separate checks.

How do you check the price?

The pricing page has the current euro and USD viewing amounts. Ask for the final quote in the currency you’ll pay, including extra screens or any separate player activation. Your payment provider may apply its own conversion or fee.

Compatible activation is included for one year with six-month and one-year plans, and for lifetime with two-year and three-year plans. The one-month plan has no free activation. The app licence doesn’t extend the viewing period.

Keep the quote, screen allowance and viewing start and expiry dates together. LoopMint renews when you ask; read the plan terms and refund policy before paying.

Copy this into your request

“My WhatsApp number uses [country code], but I’ll watch in [viewing country] on [device model]. I’d like a 24-hour trial to check [programme or category]. Please confirm the app, trial period and full offer.” Send it to Sales & Setup.
