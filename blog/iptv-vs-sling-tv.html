IPTV vs Sling TV: channels, screens and plan terms

Sling TV delivers television over the internet too. The useful comparison is between its exact package and the separate viewing offer you are considering: what you can watch, on which screens, and on what terms.

Scope and sources: LoopMint sells viewing plans and has a commercial interest in this comparison. We checked Sling’s linked public pages and our published terms on 13 November 2026. We have not compared playback on paid accounts, verified equivalent channel rights, or assigned either offer a quality rating. LoopMint is independent of Sling.

The short answer: “IPTV” describes internet television, not one competing package. Start with a named provider and plan. Sling has published package-specific rules; a separate player such as IBO Player needs its own viewing source. Owning that player does not supply a Sling subscription or its channels.

The sections below explain the limits behind this summary. None of these differences establishes that the two services carry the same programmes.

1. Check your viewing country first

Sling’s location guidance restricts streaming to the 50 United States and Puerto Rico. If you live in the UK or Ireland, do not assume a Sling account or a different player changes that restriction.

For LoopMint, accepting a request worldwide does not confirm a particular channel or app everywhere. Send your viewing country and device to Sales & Setup; use the availability guide to prepare the request.

2. Match channels and local stations to your address

List the actual programmes, competitions and local stations you need. Sling’s plan comparison separates its packages and extras. A sports label alone does not tell you whether your chosen match is included.

For local TV, Sling’s ZIP-code checker matters: local stations vary by area and package, and Sling Orange does not include local channels. An antenna can be a separate option where reception allows; it is not the same as an internet channel in your subscription.

For any other offer, including ours, ask who provides the content and what applies in your country. A channel count, broadcaster logo or working trial is not proof of a distribution agreement. We have not independently verified matching rights between LoopMint and Sling, so this guide does not promise an equivalent replacement.

3. Count simultaneous streams, not installed apps

Sling’s screen rules give Orange one stream and Blue up to three. Orange & Blue can provide four in total, but only one active device can watch its restricted Orange channels at a time. On-demand and DVR playback also use a stream.

Example: two people wanting ESPN at the same time should check that channel’s limit before buying a combined package. “Four streams” does not mean four copies of every channel.

LoopMint’s published offer begins with one active connection. If two televisions will play at once, include both in your order and get the total confirmed. An app installed on several devices, or an activated player, does not add viewing connections.

4. Separate the app licence from the viewing plan

Sling lists Fire TV, Roku, Android/Google TV, Apple and other routes on its supported-device page. Check your exact model and app-store availability before choosing a plan.

A separate IPTV player can have a device licence as well as a viewing account. LoopMint’s six-month and one-year plans include one-year activation for a compatible player; the two-year and three-year plans include lifetime player activation. The one-month plan has activation available separately. Lifetime player activation does not extend the viewing plan.

Use our player and subscription guide, or the IBO Player setup guide if that is your app. Neither guide makes a third-party player a way to access Sling.

5. Check DVR and catch-up separately

Sling’s DVR documentation describes cloud recording, including 50 hours with a subscription and an optional storage upgrade. Some channels cannot be recorded. Confirm storage, expiry and the programmes you can actually record before relying on it.

A programme guide, catch-up catalogue and recording button are different features. For LoopMint, ask whether your exact app and plan support the one you need. If recording matters, test scheduling, playback and expiry rather than assuming every device has a cloud DVR.

6. Compare the amount due and the renewal terms

For Sling, use the current checkout quote for the base package, extras, recording upgrade and applicable charges. Note the regular renewal amount as well as any introductory offer. For LoopMint, use the published plan totals and include any extra screens or separate player activation.

A cost example from our offer: the annual viewing plan is €85 paid upfront. Dividing that by 12 gives about €7.08 per month, but you still pay €85 at the start. The one-month new-client offer is €12, with later months at €17. This is not a like-for-like Sling saving calculation: content, currency, screens and commitments differ.

Sling explains how to stop a recurring monthly plan in its cancellation guide; deleting the app does not cancel billing. LoopMint’s terms specify manual renewal. Read our separate refund policy before paying; do not assume either provider’s cancellation terms apply to the other.

Which option fits your household?

  • You need US local stations: start with Sling’s ZIP-code and package results. Check the specific station, not just “local TV”.
  • Two people watch at once: write down both programmes and confirm the simultaneous-stream allowance for those channels.
  • You already own an activated player: confirm the viewing account, compatible login method and connection allowance separately.
  • You want to record live programmes: verify recording rights, storage and expiry for the actual service and device.

Test the remote, search, subtitles and required programmes on your own connection where a trial is offered. Our 24-hour trial checklist helps you keep notes. A successful trial session cannot establish long-term reliability, and this document comparison cannot establish a playback winner.
