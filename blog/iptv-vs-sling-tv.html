IPTV vs Sling TV: which is right for you?

Sling TV is one named service; IPTV describes a delivery method. If you’re weighing Sling against LoopMint, compare the plans you would actually buy.

Request a free 24-hour trialCompare plans

Published by LoopMint · 1 October 2026 · Updated 9 October 2026

About this comparison: LoopMint sells viewing plans. This guide uses Sling’s official help pages and our own published offer. We haven’t carried out a comparative playback test or assigned ratings.

Start with where you watch

Location can settle the first question quickly. Sling says its streaming service is available within the 50 United States and Puerto Rico. Its location support page explains that restriction.

LoopMint accepts trial and plan requests worldwide. Give us the country where you’ll watch so the team can check the available options and suitable app. Accepting requests worldwide doesn’t mean that every programme or player is available in every location.

Compare the exact content and plan

Write down the programmes you don’t want to lose: perhaps a local channel, a particular competition or a series. Then compare that list with the offer for your address. Two packages that both advertise “sports” may cover different events.

Sling’s packages and extras have their own channel line-ups. Use its official channel information rather than relying on an old comparison. For LoopMint, send Sales & Setup your list and viewing country. Get a current answer about the items that matter to you.

Check devices, apps and simultaneous viewing

Which television or streaming device will you use? Check Sling’s app support for that exact model. With LoopMint, confirm the compatible player and its activation term. The player licence and the viewing plan may be separate.

Next, think about the busiest evening in your household. If one person watches sport while another watches a film, you need the simultaneous-screen rules, not just a list of devices on which the app installs. Sling’s multi-device guidance sets out its plan-dependent rules; ask LoopMint for the screen allowance and any extra-screen total for your order.

Decide with a real test and full price

Add up the amount you would actually pay. Pay attention to introductory pricing, the billing period and renewal. A multi-month price divided into a monthly equivalent isn’t the same commitment as a monthly bill.

If a trial is available, use the television and connection you intend to keep using. Try the remote, search and programmes on your list. That gives you evidence about your setup; a generic “best provider” label doesn’t.
