IPTV vs Sling TV: which is right for you?

“IPTV” describes a way television can be delivered over the internet; it is not one provider or one standard package. Sling TV is a named subscription service with its own plans and rules.

About this comparison: LoopMint sells viewing plans. This is a practical comparison guide, not a claim that we tested or ranked either service.

Start with where you watch

First, check whether the service is available where you live. Sling says its streaming service is available in the 50 United States and Puerto Rico. LoopMint accepts enquiries worldwide, but the app and viewing options still depend on your country and device.

See Sling’s location support page for its current explanation, then confirm the terms directly before subscribing.

Compare the exact content and plan

Don’t compare services by logo walls or headline channel counts. Make a short list of the channels, matches, films or series you actually care about, then check whether each is included in the plan for your location. Sling has different packages and extras, and local channels or sports can depend on the plan and where you watch.

Check Sling’s official channel guide for its current line-up. For LoopMint, ask Sales & Setup to confirm the available options for your country.

Check devices, apps and simultaneous viewing

Check the app as well as the service. Sling has its own app; some other providers ask you to use a separate player, which may have its own activation terms. Look up your exact TV or streaming-device model in the official app store before paying.

How many screens can watch at once? Sling’s answer depends on the plan and extras; its multi-device rules explain the current limits. Ask LoopMint about the connection limit on the plan you’re considering. Having an app on two TVs doesn’t tell you whether both can stream at the same time.

Decide with a real test and full price

Now compare the amount due today, plan length, renewal, app fees and support. A monthly equivalent is useful, but don’t mistake it for a monthly payment if the provider charges for several months upfront. If there’s a trial, use your own device and home connection at the time you normally watch.

We sell LoopMint plans, so we’re not presenting this as an independent ranking. Use the trial guide, check LoopMint’s current prices, and read how we review services before making up your mind.
