IPTV vs Sling TV: which is right for you?

“IPTV” describes a way television can be delivered over the internet; it is not one provider or one standard package. Sling TV is a named subscription service with its own plans and rules.

Start with where you watch

Sling says its content is available for streaming in the 50 United States and Puerto Rico. If you watch elsewhere, check a service's country availability before comparing channel lists. LoopMint accepts enquiries from around the world, but content and app availability still vary by location and need confirmation for your address.

Source: Sling's location support page. Check the current service terms yourself before subscribing.

Compare the exact content and plan

Sling publishes different base packages, extras and channel lineups. Its local channels and sports access can depend on the plan and location. An IPTV-labelled offer can differ just as much between providers. List the specific channels, sports events and on-demand titles you need; then verify each against the current offer for your country.

Use Sling's official plan guide for its current options. Ask LoopMint Sales & Setup to confirm its current options and rights for your location. A logo wall is not a reliable comparison.

Check devices, apps and simultaneous viewing

Sling uses its own app, while another internet TV service may require a compatible separate player. That app may have its own activation term. Check the exact TV or streaming-device model and official app-store availability before paying.

Sling says simultaneous streams depend on its chosen base plan and extras; see its current multi-device rules. With LoopMint, ask how many simultaneous connections the viewing plan permits. A player installed on several screens does not change the service's connection limit.

Decide with a real test and full price

Compare the current upfront payment, renewal method, optional extras, app activation and support. Do not compare a promotional monthly amount with a different plan's multi-month upfront price. If you can test a service, use your own screen and normal internet connection at the time you usually watch.
