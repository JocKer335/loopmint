IBO PLAYER SETUP

IBO Player setup and activation: playlist and login help

Start by checking the app’s full name and developer. Install the matching player, add the viewing details for your own account, then check whether its app licence needs activation.

About this guide: LoopMint sells viewing plans and provides setup help. This guide follows the linked publisher instructions and public forms checked on 12 November 2026; it is not a device test or an endorsement by an app developer.

Which IBO Player app do you have?

Before following a download link or paying for activation, open the app’s About, Help or activation screen. Record the full title, developer, version and website shown there. On the store listing, compare the publisher as well as the name. Keep the device model and viewing country handy.

The IBO DEV licence store lists an IBO Player product. The IBO PLAYER listing on Apple’s App Store identifies its seller as ibrahim akoum and directs users to iboiptv.com. The Ibo Player Pro website publishes another setup route. A search for “IBO Player Pro” or “IBO Pro Player” can therefore show instructions that do not match the app on your screen.

The steps below describe the iboplayer.com and IBO DEV route. Use that route only when it matches your installed app. If your app names a different website, follow that publisher’s instructions or ask support to identify it before entering device details.

Find the right IBO Player download

  1. Check your TV or streaming device’s app store for the exact app and publisher you identified.
  2. If it is listed for your device, install it there and open it on the screen you intend to use.
  3. If it is not listed, use the matching publisher’s current installation instructions. For the iboplayer.com route, consult its downloads page.
  4. Confirm that the app opens before arranging a licence or viewing plan. Note any error exactly as it appears.

IBO Player on Fire TV or Fire Stick

Check the Fire TV model and Fire OS version alongside the app name. A mobile Android listing alone does not establish that its menus work with your Fire TV remote. Follow the publisher’s Fire TV instructions for that exact app, then test navigation, search and playback controls. Our Fire TV setup guide covers the device checks.

If the publisher supplies a download address or Downloader code, get the current one from its own page. A code copied from an old video may point to a different version. If you already have a working installation, identify it before replacing it.

IBO Player login: which details belong where?

Three separate parts of the setup
What you are openingWhat to check
Player’s device portalUse the app identity and device details requested by the matching publisher.
Your viewing accountUse your provider’s playlist or its server, username and password, following the chosen setup method.
App licenceCheck the player and device covered by the activation, and the licence term.

Copy an identifier from the app’s own screen. Do not substitute a router’s address or another device’s details unless the publisher specifically asks for them. Keep device keys, passwords, payment references and private playlist links out of public screenshots.

How to add your IBO Player playlist

For an app using the iboplayer.com route, use its Manage Playlist portal or the matching IBO DEV Add Playlist form. Check the app and website match before continuing.

  1. Open the player and note its displayed MAC or device ID and key.
  2. Select the correct application where the portal asks for a product. Fill the required device fields; different forms may ask for different details.
  3. Choose the playlist method supplied by your viewing provider. Keep a playlist URL separate from a server-and-login setup.
  4. Enter a recognisable playlist name and your own account’s details in the matching fields. Check for copied spaces or missing characters.
  5. Save the entry, then reopen the player and check that the playlist appears.
  6. Try a known item and the controls you normally use before changing other settings.

The server-and-login form separately requests a host, username and password. Use that form only when it matches your provider’s setup details. A successful portal login does not establish that the viewing account or its playlist is still active.

How to check IBO Player activation

Installing the app, activating its licence and adding viewing access are separate steps. The IBO DEV IBO Player product page describes an app trial followed by a paid licence; it does not include channels or a viewing subscription.

  1. Read the licence message in the installed player and identify which device it refers to.
  2. For the matching IBO DEV app, use Check Mac to select the application and check the displayed device address.
  3. If a licence is needed, confirm the app, device and term before payment. Keep the confirmation and reopen the player afterwards.
  4. If it still asks for activation, ask the seller to check that transaction against the same app and device before paying again.

For LoopMint viewing plans, compatible player activation is included for one year with six-month and one-year plans, and for lifetime with two-year and three-year plans. The one-month plan includes no free player activation. Confirm the exact app and included term with Sales & Setup before arranging a separate licence. Lifetime describes the app licence under its publisher’s terms; the viewing plan still has its own expiry date. See the included activation terms and current plans.

When setup or activation is not working

The portal rejects the MAC or device key

Compare the website with the one shown by your app, then check the selected product and each character of the device details. Say whether the failure happens before the portal opens or after a playlist is saved. Hide the actual key when sharing the error publicly.

The app opens, but the playlist is empty

Check whether your saved playlist appears at all. If it does not, review the selected device and entry. If it appears but has no working items, ask your viewing provider to confirm the account status and playlist details. Repeated app-licence payments do not diagnose that problem.

You paid, but the player still says “Activate”

Compare the device and app on the receipt with the current installation. Ask the party that handled the licence to check its status. Before reinstalling, resetting or moving to another device, confirm what happens to the existing licence and playlist.

Viewing starts, then freezes

Record whether it affects one item or everything, the time it happened and the connection you were using. Try the playback checks one at a time. For an existing LoopMint viewing account, send the app, model and error to Technical Support; for installation and initial activation, use Sales & Setup.

Common setup questions

Does downloading IBO Player include TV channels?

The linked IBO DEV product describes a media player without included content. Arrange your own authorised viewing source separately. An app trial and a viewing trial can have different terms and end dates.

Can I use these steps for IBO Player Pro?

First match the full store title, publisher and website. The Pro site linked above describes its own playlist and in-app activation workflow. Use the instructions for your installed app rather than assuming its portal accepts another app’s device key.

Need help identifying your player?

Tell LoopMint Sales & Setup the app’s full name and developer, your device model, viewing country and the message on screen. Keep private credentials out of that first enquiry. We can then check the supported setup and what your plan includes.

IBO Player playlist URL or separate login?

Look at the field labels before pasting anything. If the form asks for one playlist URL, keep the complete provider-supplied link together, including its account parameters. If it asks separately for a host, username and password, use the matching server-and-login route linked above. A playlist name is a label you choose; it is not the viewing username.

If your source loads categories but playback fails, record that result before changing the login. If the portal rejects the device key before opening, check the app identity and device fields first. These failures happen at different steps, so a new app-licence payment does not address both.

Moving to another player? Follow the BOB Player, SmartOne IPTV or TiviMate guide for that product’s own setup. Do not reuse an IBO device key in another app’s portal.
