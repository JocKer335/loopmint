TiviMate setup: playlists, login and Premium explained

TiviMate needs your own viewing source. Start with the correct TV app, choose the playlist format your provider supplied and keep your Premium account separate from your viewing login.

About this guide: LoopMint sells viewing plans and provides setup help. The linked publisher pages were reviewed on 13 November 2026. This is a setup reference, not a hands-on device test or a claim of affiliation with the developer. Availability and menus can change.

Install the right TiviMate app

The TiviMate IPTV Player listing on Google Play names Armobsoft FZE as the developer. Follow the links from tivimate.com to find the matching release. The publisher also offers its APK at tivimate.com/apk.

The publisher describes the app as intended for Android TV and not optimised for phones or tablets. Do not interpret a search for “TiviMate Windows” or “TiviMate PC” as evidence of an official desktop edition. The linked publisher material does not establish a native Windows or macOS player.

For Fire TV, first confirm whether your exact model and operating system support the publisher’s Android installation route. Use the current publisher address rather than an old third-party code. Check remote navigation before paying; our Fire TV guide covers the model and installation checks.

M3U or Xtream Codes: choose the details you were given

The developer’s app listing identifies M3U, Xtream Codes and Stalker Portal support. Start with the format provided for your authorised account; this walkthrough focuses on the first two. Menu wording can differ by version.

Keep playlist formats separate
Setup choiceDetails to prepare
M3U playlistThe complete playlist URL supplied for your account; an EPG URL if separately provided.
Xtream CodesThe provider’s server address, username and password, entered in their matching fields.
TiviMate Premium accountThe app account used for Premium. This is separate from either viewing method above.
  1. Open the player and choose its add-playlist option.
  2. Select the format that matches your setup message.
  3. Enter the full URL or the separate server and credentials, as appropriate. Check copied characters before continuing.
  4. Let the playlist load and give it a recognisable name if prompted.
  5. Open a known item and check sound, channel changes and the remote’s Back control.

If you have only a server login, do not paste it into the playlist-URL field. Ask your provider for the missing format rather than putting private credentials into a public online converter.

Premium, Companion and your viewing subscription

TiviMate is a player without included channels. The publisher’s purchase terms distinguish a lifetime app purchase from auto-renewing subscriptions; the options, billing period and price are shown at purchase. Neither supplies a viewing package.

The developer’s TiviMate Companion listing describes it as a tool for unlocking Premium on devices without Google Play and managing activated devices. Companion itself is not a TV player. Use the current purchase or restore route shown by your installed player and publisher account.

If Premium appears missing, check which account and purchase route were used and retain the receipt. Do not buy again merely because the viewing playlist is empty. For any LoopMint activation offer, get this exact product confirmed with Sales & Setup; the plan’s app terms are not a blanket promise to cover every Premium purchase.

The playlist works, but the TV guide is empty

Programme information and the playable stream are separate things to check. Ask the provider whether your account includes a current EPG source and whether it matches the playlist’s channel identifiers. If an EPG link was supplied, compare it with the entry saved in the app before changing time-offset settings.

Check the device date and time, then note whether the guide is empty everywhere or only for some channels. A blank schedule alone does not show that a new app licence is needed. Catch-up and recording also depend on the source, permissions and device capabilities; their presence in a menu does not establish availability for every programme.

A useful TiviMate troubleshooting report

Record the app version, device model, playlist format and exact error. Say whether the list fails to load, loads without a schedule, or plays and then stops. Change one thing at a time so you can tell which step affected the result.

For existing LoopMint viewing access, contact Technical Support. For app-account or Premium receipt problems, use the support route on the publisher’s site. Never post your full playlist URL, server password or account receipt in a public comment.

If the programme guide is empty or shifted in time, follow the EPG troubleshooting checks. For choosing login details, compare M3U and Xtream setup.
