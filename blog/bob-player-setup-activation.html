BOB PLAYER SETUP

BOB Player setup and activation: download and playlist help

BOB Player installation, device-portal login and viewing access are separate steps. Use the publisher route for your exact app, then check where setup stops before buying another activation.

Published by LoopMint · 13 November 2026 · Updated 13 November 2026

About this guide: LoopMint sells viewing plans and provides setup help. The linked publisher pages were reviewed on 13 November 2026. This is a setup reference, not a hands-on device test or a claim of affiliation with the developer. Availability and menus can change.

Find your BOB Player download

Use bobplayer.com as your starting point and compare its links with the website shown in your app. The publisher’s download page lists routes for Tizen TVs, LG TVs, Android and iOS. Select the route for your screen, then verify availability for your model and store country.

BOB Player, BOB Pro and BOB Premium names should not be assumed to share device keys or licence terms. Keep the full store title and developer in a support enquiry. Replacing a working installation with a similarly named result may make the original playlist and purchase harder to identify.

BOB Player on Fire TV

A search for “BOB Player download Fire TV” does not prove that a particular release runs on your model. Confirm your operating system and a publisher-supported installation route before using an APK. Our Fire TV guide explains the model checks. Do not rely on an unverified download code from a comment or video.

Log in to Manage Your Playlist

The publisher’s Manage Your Playlist portal requests a MAC address and device key, plus a captcha. These identify the player installation; they are not your viewing provider’s username and password.

  1. Open BOB Player on the device you plan to watch.
  2. Read the displayed MAC and device key, keeping both private.
  3. Compare the website in the app with the publisher portal before entering them.
  4. Complete the required device fields and captcha, then log in.
  5. Use the playlist controls displayed in your portal to add the authorised source supplied for your own account. Follow the field labels for that version.
  6. Save, reopen the TV app and check whether the saved source appears.

If your provider supplied separate credentials but your portal requests a single playlist URL, ask for the matching format. Do not put the device key in the source-password field or publish the full URL while asking for help.

What does BOB Player activation cover?

The BOB Player FAQ says activation does not include a playlist or channels. Test your own source before purchasing the app. Keep the device identity, seller confirmation and term so that a later licence enquiry can be checked against the same installation.

For LoopMint, compatible app activation is included for one year with six-month and one-year viewing plans, and lifetime with two-year and three-year plans. The one-month plan includes no free activation. Ask Sales & Setup to confirm this exact BOB product and device before paying separately. Lifetime describes the app licence under publisher terms, not permanent viewing access.

If the app still requests activation after payment, first ask the licence seller to compare the recorded device with the one currently shown. A new payment should not be the default troubleshooting step.

Playlist, connection and playback problems

The MAC changed after switching networks

The publisher’s FAQ discusses different Wi-Fi and Ethernet MAC addresses on TVs. If the problem began after changing the connection type, record that change and compare the current app address with the saved device. Follow the publisher’s guidance for that product rather than substituting a router address.

The app waits at startup

The same FAQ explains that BOB loads its list from the source server at startup. Record whether it eventually loads or produces an error. Ask the provider to check the source’s availability before assuming the app licence expired.

It works on a computer, but not the television

BOB’s support notes identify device-format differences as one possibility. Compare the same item on the same connection, then note the TV model and whether sound, picture or both are missing. A working computer test alone does not establish that the TV supports the source format.

The programme guide is missing

Ask whether the provider’s source includes compatible EPG information. Loading a playlist is not proof that schedule data is available for each item.

For an existing LoopMint viewing account, send the model, app version, exact error and a working/failing example to Technical Support. For a portal rejection, describe the field that fails without posting the device key publicly.
