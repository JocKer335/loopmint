SMARTERS PRO SETUP

IPTV Smarters Pro setup: downloads, login and device help

Searching for IPTV Smarters Pro? Match the publisher and version first. This guide covers the current Smarters Pro route, the details you need to sign in and the checks to make before paying.

About this guide: LoopMint sells viewing plans and provides setup help. The Fire Stick instructions were checked against Amazon and the publisher on 28 November 2026. This is a setup reference, not a hands-on device test or a claim of affiliation with the developer. Availability and menus can change.

Which Smarters app are you installing?

Similar names in search results can refer to different products. Open the store listing from the publisher’s website, then compare its developer and support address with your installed app. A familiar icon or the word “Pro” is not enough to establish the same software.

The current Smarters Pro publisher page identifies its Samsung app’s developer as TECH SMARTERS PRIVATE LIMITED. These instructions do not establish that every older app called IPTV Smarters Pro uses the same account or installation route. Keep the full app title, version and device model when asking for help.

Choose a download for your actual device

Start from the download links on the publisher’s installation page, which distinguishes Android TV, Android phone and iOS versions. A phone installation is not proof of compatibility with a television remote.

When setting up Smarters Pro in Ireland, open the store on the device you plan to watch on. If more than one app has a similar name, compare the publisher with the setup instructions before entering your viewing credentials.

How do I find IPTV Smarters Pro on my Fire Stick?

On your Fire Stick, open Find on the home screen and search for Smarters Pro. If you are in Ireland, use the Amazon account and stick you plan to watch with so you see the listings available to you.

  1. Identify your stick. In Settings, open My Fire TV (or Device & Software), then About. Write down the full model name, including labels such as 4K Select or 4K Max.
  2. Open the app details. Check the developer and support website against the Smarters Pro publisher page. Check the listing’s Works With details for your model. Amazon explains where to find compatibility information in its Fire TV model guide.
  3. If the publisher and model match, select Get if offered. Once installed, open the player and follow the playlist and login steps below. You can also follow Amazon’s app download instructions.

If the app is missing or the developer differs, ask Smarters Pro support for the Amazon listing for your exact model and country. We could not verify a direct Amazon Appstore link for this release in the publisher’s downloads. If you need another player, use the Fire TV setup checklist to find one your viewing provider supports.

Smarters Pro login and playlist setup

The publisher’s How It Works guide says to obtain subscription credentials from your service provider. Its player also supports M3U and JSON sources. The fields available depend on the installed version; follow the method shown in that app rather than forcing instructions from another release.

  1. Open the verified app and read the available source or sign-in choices.
  2. Compare those choices with the setup message for your own authorised viewing account.
  3. If asked for a playlist URL, copy the complete supplied link. If the chosen method asks for a server and separate credentials, keep each value in its own field.
  4. Give the source a name you will recognise, then save or continue using the app’s displayed controls.
  5. Check whether categories load, then try one known item before adjusting playback settings.

Do not enter your email-account password in a viewing-password field. If your provider supplied one format and the app requests another, ask for the matching details; changing only the server spelling is unlikely to resolve a format mismatch.

Does Smarters Pro Premium include channels?

No. The publisher’s Premium notice separates extra app features from media access. Buying an upgrade does not supply a viewing subscription. Confirm which optional features you need and the terms shown at checkout.

For a LoopMint plan, ask Sales & Setup whether this exact app and device are supported and whether an app activation is included. A plan’s activation offer should not be assumed to cover every player’s Premium purchase. The activation terms explain the distinction.

When login or playback fails

Login is rejected immediately

Write down the exact error and the field names, without sharing their contents publicly. Check copied spaces, the source format and whether your viewing account has expired. A successful publisher-account login does not confirm an active viewing account.

Categories load but one programme will not play

Try a second known item and record whether the fault affects all content or just one source. This separates an account-wide failure from an item-specific problem. Avoid repeatedly reinstalling before recording the working setup.

Who should you contact?

The Smarters Pro support page handles application problems, rather than content from external providers. For an existing LoopMint account, send the model, app version and error to Technical Support. Include the time and affected item, but keep playlist links and passwords private.
