ON-DEMAND VIEWING

How to watch films and series online

A large catalogue number tells you little about the films and series you want to watch. Search for specific titles on your own device before committing to a plan.

A note from us: LoopMint sells viewing plans. These tips are meant to help you compare providers, including us.

Search for titles, not just a catalogue count

Before comparing film and series libraries, make a short list of the titles you’d actually watch. Search for the film or series, and for a show check that the seasons and episodes you want are there. See whether each title is included in the plan or costs extra. Libraries change, so a number on a sales page can’t answer those questions.

If a particular title is a deal-breaker, ask the provider to confirm it for your country before paying. A promotional image or studio logo isn’t proof that it’s included.

Check the experience on your screen

Try the app on the TV, phone or computer you use most. Search for a title, open an episode, pause and resume, and check subtitles and language options. If other people in your home will use it, see whether favourites, profiles or parental controls are available.

Your screen’s resolution doesn’t guarantee the stream will be in 4K; the source and internet connection matter too. The 24-hour trial checklist can help you test the setup at the time you normally watch.

Separate the player from the content

The app and the viewing plan aren’t always the same purchase. A player can organise categories and play streams, but activating it doesn’t give you access to a film library. Ask whether your viewing service uses a separate app and whether activation is included. Our app versus service guide explains the difference.

LoopMint offers live and on-demand options, but the available titles can vary with your country, device, app and plan. Ask Sales & Setup about any title you want to watch.

Compare the full commitment

Compare the amount due now, the plan length, renewal and support. If you mainly watch one film library, a dedicated licensed service may suit you better. If you want a mix of live TV, films and series, try the categories you care about instead of choosing by the biggest catalogue number.
