SET IPTV SETUP

SET IPTV setup and activation: add your own playlist

SETIPTV connects a playlist to the MAC shown in the app. Use the matching SETSYSTEME website, load the right source format and reopen the player to check the result.

Start with your appAdd your playlist

Published by LoopMint · 13 November 2026 · Updated 13 November 2026

About this guide: LoopMint sells viewing plans and provides setup help. The linked publisher pages were reviewed on 13 November 2026. This is a setup reference, not a hands-on device test or a claim of affiliation with the developer. Availability and menus can change.

Check SETIPTV and your screen first

This guide follows SETIPTV by SETSYSTEME at setsysteme.com. Match that website with the app’s own Help or activation screen. A search result for another product containing “SET”, “Pro” or “IPTV” is not enough to establish the same setup route.

The publisher’s English FAQ lists compatible Smart TVs, Android TV devices and Android tablets. Check your full model, operating system and regional app store before installing. There is no reason to buy a licence for a device where you have not confirmed the matching app opens.

For television model checks, use our Smart TV setup guide. If you are changing devices, check the licence rules first rather than expecting a saved MAC to transfer.

How to add a SET IPTV playlist

The SETIPTV FAQ supports a provider’s M3U link or Xtream Codes details: server address, username and password. Use the format supplied for your own authorised source. The MAC displayed by the app selects the device that should receive it.

  1. Open SETIPTV on your TV and note the MAC shown there.
  2. On a phone or computer, open the publisher’s Set up my playlists page.
  3. Enter the app’s MAC and follow the form for the source format you received.
  4. For an M3U entry, keep the whole supplied URL together. For the separate-login method, put the server and credentials in their matching fields.
  5. Send or save the setup using the displayed control.
  6. Close and reopen SETIPTV on the television, then select the source and try one known item.

The setup page says saved playlists appear when the app next starts. For an edit, load the existing setup first rather than overwriting it blindly. Check the source name and device before replacing a working entry.

SET IPTV activation and device changes

The publisher separates the player licence from content: SETIPTV does not supply channels or a viewing subscription. Its FAQ says activation is tied to the device MAC and cannot be moved to another device. Confirm the intended television before paying, and check the current terms on the activation page.

A player’s test period and your viewing provider’s test period can expire on different dates. Record both. When a test ends, identify whether the screen is asking for an app licence or reporting expired viewing access.

For LoopMint viewing plans, compatible app activation is included for one year with six-month and one-year plans, and lifetime with two-year and three-year plans. The one-month plan includes no free activation. Ask Sales & Setup to confirm the exact SETIPTV app, device and available term. The publisher’s licence rules still apply, and lifetime app use does not mean a lifetime viewing subscription.

When the playlist does not appear or will not play

No saved source appears on the TV

Compare the MAC in the submitted form with the current app screen, then close and reopen the player. Check whether the form actually confirmed saving. A typo in the device address can attach an otherwise valid entry to the wrong setup.

The source is listed but empty

Ask your viewing provider to verify the complete source link and account status. Explain whether the app shows categories, an empty list or an explicit error. Re-entering only the username will not resolve a missing or incorrectly copied URL.

One item fails while others work

Record the item name, time and whether the failure affects picture, sound or both. Compare a second item before resetting the app. Keep the original source details available privately so you can restore them if a reset becomes necessary.

What should I send support?

Share the full app title, device model, viewing country and error wording first. Say whether the issue began after editing a source, changing the network or replacing a device.
