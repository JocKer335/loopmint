What should a review tell you?

It should show what was tested, on which device, in what country and when. It should also be clear about what the reviewer couldn’t confirm, instead of repeating a provider’s sales claims as fact.

Viewing options can change by country, plan and date. If you care about a particular channel, film or match, check it for your location before you pay. Our reviews will say where the test took place and which details still need checking.
