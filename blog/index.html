What a useful review should tell you

A review should say what was tested, where and when, on which device, and what could not be confirmed. It should separate a provider's marketing claims from observations made during a real trial.

Content rights and availability may differ across the UK, US, Ireland and other countries. A named channel, film or sporting event should never be assumed to be included everywhere. Our articles will identify the country tested and flag any claim that still needs confirmation.
