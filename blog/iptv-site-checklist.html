BUYER'S GUIDE

12 checks before you subscribe to an IPTV site

A polished website can make any service look convincing. These checks help you find the facts that matter before you start a trial or pay for a plan.

Published by LoopMint · 25 September 2026

Disclosure: LoopMint publishes this guide and sells viewing plans. Apply the same checks to LoopMint as you would to any other provider. This is a buying checklist, not a review or endorsement of a named website.

The useful question is not how many logos or catalogue numbers a site displays. It is what the provider can confirm for your country, your device and the plan you would actually buy.

Website and offer

  1. Who is selling the service? Look for a clear business or trading name, working contact route and a way to resolve a problem after payment. An anonymous sales page gives you little to follow up on.
  2. What exactly is included? Ask whether the price covers a content service, a separate player app, or both. A compatible app does not mean its developer runs or endorses the provider.
  3. Which countries are served? Tell the provider where you will watch. Ask for the options and viewing terms that apply there. Do not assume a UK, US or Irish offer is the same in another country.
  4. Can specific content claims be checked? If a channel, match, film or series is essential, ask for current availability in your location and how the provider is authorised to offer it. Treat a logo wall or screenshot as advertising, not proof.

Trial and performance

  1. Are trial terms clear? Confirm its length, cost, start time, what it includes and whether it ends automatically. Keep the response in writing.
  2. Does it work on your own screen? Check the exact TV, stick, phone or computer model. Install recommended players from official stores or their own websites. Ask about extra app fees before paying.
  3. Have you tested your normal viewing time? Use the same device and connection you would use day to day, including a busy evening if that is when you watch. A brief demonstration on another person's device is not the same test.
  4. Are picture and access features suitable? Check sound, subtitles, programme information, navigation and parental controls if you need them. Quality can vary by source, device and connection.

Price, support and data

  1. What is the total price? Compare the amount due now, the plan period, simultaneous screens and any device or app charges. A monthly equivalent may still require a larger upfront payment.
  2. What happens at expiry? Check the exact renewal method, whether you must ask to renew, and any cancellation or refund terms. Save a copy of the terms offered to you.
  3. Can support answer a real question? Ask about your device or a country-specific availability issue during the trial. Judge whether the answer is clear and useful, not only how quickly it arrives.
  4. How are your details handled? Read the site's privacy information before sharing a name, phone number or payment details. Ask who receives your information and how to request its removal.

Make the comparison fair

Write down the same answers for each provider: country, device, trial result, total price, renewal terms and support response. Our review method explains the evidence a named review should show. For a practical test schedule, use the 24-hour trial checklist.
