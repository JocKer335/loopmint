BUYER'S GUIDE

Best IPTV service? 12 checks before you choose

A good-looking website is a starting point. These twelve questions help you check the actual offer, try it at home and decide whether it suits you.

The best choice for a sports viewer with two televisions may be a poor fit for someone who mainly watches films on a tablet. Start with your own country, screen and must-have programmes. Then keep the answers to these questions together, rather than trying to remember each sales page.

Website and offer

  1. Who are you paying? Look for a business or trading name and a working contact route. Check where you would go with a problem after payment, not just where to send a sales enquiry.
  2. What is the product? Is the payment for viewing access, a player licence or both? Our app and service guide explains the difference. Installing a player doesn’t tell you who supplies the content.
  3. Does the offer apply where you watch? Give your viewing country. A country code in a phone number or an example from another customer doesn’t establish the terms for your location.
  4. Is the programme you want actually available? Name the channel, event, film or series. Ask about the current listing and any restrictions. A page of logos can’t answer that question.

Trial and performance

  1. When does the trial start and finish? Ask what it includes, whether it costs anything and whether it leads to a paid renewal. Save the answer before activating access.
  2. Which app works on your device? Send the full model, not just “Smart TV.” Use the supported app’s official store or developer download, and check any activation requirement.
  3. Have you tried your normal setup? Use the screen and connection you’ll keep using. Test during your usual viewing time, especially if several people share the connection then.
  4. Do the everyday controls work for you? Try sound, subtitles, search, remote navigation and programme information. If you need parental controls, find out whether the particular app provides them.

Price, support and data

  1. What is the full amount due today? Note the upfront payment, plan length, app activation and extra-screen charges. A monthly equivalent describes value over time; it may not be the bill you pay each month.
  2. What happens at expiry? Check whether renewal is automatic or requested. Read the refund and cancellation terms while you still have time to compare them.
  3. Can support answer a real question? Ask about your setup during the trial. Keep the reply. A useful answer tells you more than a promise of “24/7 support.”
  4. Where does your information go? Read the privacy page before sharing a number or payment details. Look for an explanation of who receives the data and how to request changes or removal.

Turn the answers into a decision

For each provider, write down the same five things: what you wanted to watch, what worked in the trial, the total price, renewal terms and the support answer. Mark unanswered questions clearly. A gap in your notes is a reason to ask again before paying.
