IPTV EPG not working? Check a missing or incorrect TV guide

A blank programme guide and a stream that will not play are different problems. Check whether the listings are empty, incomplete or shifted in time before changing the playlist.

Start with the checksHelp centre

Published by LoopMint · 19 November 2026 · Updated 25 November 2026

About this guide: LoopMint sells viewing plans and provides setup help. Publisher documentation was reviewed on 19 November 2026. These are diagnostic steps, not a hands-on test of every app or device, and they do not imply developer affiliation.

What does EPG mean in IPTV?

EPG means electronic programme guide: the programme names and times shown beside channels. Those listings are separate information from the video itself. A channel can play while its guide is blank, and a visible schedule does not guarantee that its stream will play.

Before changing settings, choose one channel with a missing guide and one with working listings. Note the date and whether the stream plays. If video is freezing as well, use the buffering checks for that symptom instead of assuming an EPG refresh will repair playback.

SymptomWhat to record

Listings show yesterdayThe date range in the source and whether refresh completes.
Times are consistently shiftedDevice time zone, local time and any manual guide offset.

Check the guide source before replacing it

Open the guide or EPG settings for the exact app and playlist you use. Menu names differ between players and versions. Record the source and last-update status privately. If the app offers a guide refresh, run it once and let it finish; note the result before repeating it.

If your player asks for an XMLTV address, ask your viewing provider for the matching guide feed. The XMLTV project’s format definition describes programme listings linked to channel identifiers and broadcast times. A feed containing different channel identifiers may not match the playlist you loaded.

Do not assume that a playlist URL and a guide URL are interchangeable. With some supported account-login methods, the player may obtain guide details through the account; other setups require a separate feed. Confirm the method for your actual app and provider. The M3U and Xtream comparison explains which details to ask for without promising the same behaviour in every player.

Only some channels have no EPG

If most listings work, avoid deleting the whole playlist. Give the provider the names of two missing channels and one that works. Mention region or channel variant where shown: similarly named services can have different schedules.

Ask whether the provider’s guide covers those channels and whether their identifiers match the list. If the player offers manual assignment, use verified app instructions and the correct guide entry. Matching by a similar display name alone can attach the wrong programme schedule.

The guide shows the wrong time

Check the television or streaming device’s date, time and time zone first. Compare the guide against a programme whose scheduled start you can verify. Note whether every listing is shifted by the same amount or whether only one channel looks wrong.

For a TV in Ireland, check that its clock shows the correct local time for Dublin. Compare the guide with a programme whose start time you know before changing an offset. An offset applied in both the TV and the player can shift the guide twice.

Record any existing EPG offset before changing it. Applying a manual correction when the source or device already accounts for the time zone can introduce another shift. Make one change, refresh if required by the app, then compare the same programme again. Ask the provider about source times if the correct offset is unclear.

What support needs to investigate

  • The full player name/version and device model.
  • Whether video plays and whether the problem is all channels or a few.
  • Example channel names, the guide date and your viewing time zone.
  • The last refresh outcome and exact error message, if any.

Get help with your LoopMint guide

For an existing account, contact Technical Support with those details. Hide account credentials and private guide links in public screenshots. Do not pay for a new app licence just because programme listings are missing.

For initial player setup, choose the exact TiviMate, Smarters Pro or SET IPTV guide. This is a guide to narrowing the fault; available listings and controls depend on the app and viewing source.
