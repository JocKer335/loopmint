How much does an IPTV subscription cost?

Compare what you pay today, not just the monthly figure in large print. Here’s how the viewing plan, player activation and extra screens fit into the total.

What makes up the total cost?

A plan advertised at a few euros a month may require a full year’s payment today. That isn’t necessarily a bad offer, but you need to know which amount you’re comparing.

Start with the viewing-plan price and its length. Add any player activation you need and the cost of screens that will watch at the same time. Then check the renewal terms. A player licence is a separate product, even when it comes free with a viewing plan.

What €85 for a year actually means

LoopMint’s one-year plan is €85 upfront. Divide that by 12 and the monthly equivalent is about €7.08. You still pay €85 for the year; it isn’t a €7.08 monthly instalment.

The two-year plan is €150 upfront, or about €6.25 per month over 24 months. It lowers the equivalent rate, but commits more money at the start. Compare that commitment with what you learned during the trial, rather than choosing on the smaller monthly number alone.

The new-client one-month offer is €12 for the first month; a later one-month renewal is €17. The current pricing page is the place to check today’s viewing-plan amounts and any changed offer.

That inclusion can make a difference if your device needs a player such as EBO, where supported. Ask which app will be activated and check that it works on your model. Separate one-year and lifetime activation are also available; get the current fee from Sales & Setup if you need one.

Lifetime activation covers the app licence under the app provider’s terms. It doesn’t extend viewing access: a two-year viewing plan ends after its stated term even though the player licence is lifetime.

Compare like with like

Use a note with three amounts: due today, any later renewal and any separately payable extras. Alongside it, write the expiry date and what each payment includes. This is easier to compare than discounts calculated from different reference prices.

For two televisions, say whether both will play at once. Having the app installed on each TV doesn’t establish how many streams the viewing plan covers. Check the extra-screen total for your actual order before paying.

Questions to send before paying

  • What is the full amount due, in which currency, and when will viewing access expire?
  • Which compatible player activation comes with this plan?
  • What is the total if my household needs another simultaneous screen?
  • Do I request renewal, and which refund terms apply?
