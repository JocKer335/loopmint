How to renew your IPTV subscription with LoopMint

Continue an existing viewing plan with clear dates and a confirmed total. First check whether you need viewing renewal, player activation or help with a playback problem.

Published by LoopMint · 19 November 2026 · Updated 25 November 2026

About this guide: LoopMint sells viewing plans. These steps describe renewal of an existing LoopMint account, using our manual-renewal terms. Prices and included activation should be confirmed for the order you choose.

How to renew an IPTV subscription with LoopMint

To renew an existing LoopMint viewing plan, contact Sales & Setup, ask the team to check your account privately and choose the next plan length. Confirm the full price, viewing dates and number of simultaneous screens before paying. LoopMint plans do not renew automatically.

  1. Check what has expired. Note whether the message refers to your viewing account, your player’s licence or a playlist that failed to load. These need different checks.
  2. Identify your existing account privately. Start with your device model, viewing country and the message on screen. The team can ask for the appropriate account reference privately. Do not post passwords, device keys or complete playlist links in public comments.
  3. Choose the viewing term. Use the current plans to compare the available durations. Ask for the total for your actual setup, including any extra simultaneous screens and any separately needed player activation.
  4. Confirm dates before payment. Ask when the renewed access will start and end. If your current term has time left, ask how that remaining time will be handled rather than assuming it will be added automatically.
  5. Keep the confirmation and check playback. After the team confirms the account update, reopen your player and try a known channel or title. If you are told the existing login remains valid, keep it; use replacement details only when the team explicitly supplies them.

Ask about renewing your LoopMint plan

Tell Sales & Setup your device, country and the term you want. Ask for the account status, full total and new dates before making a payment.

Prepare a renewal message on WhatsApp

You can edit the message before sending. Opening the link does not renew the account or make a payment.

My IPTV subscription expired: what should I check?

An expired viewing account can prevent playback even if the player itself still opens. A “licence expired” notice from the app can instead refer to the player. Send the exact wording so the team can identify the right product before you buy anything.

A missing playlist, a connection error or one channel failing does not by itself establish that your plan has expired. For an interruption on an account that should still be active, follow the playback checks and contact Technical Support. Sales & Setup handles renewal and initial activation enquiries.

Does renewing viewing also renew the player licence?

Check both separately. Viewing access and a player licence can expire on different dates. A lifetime player licence does not provide lifetime viewing, and paying for viewing does not establish that every app on every device has been licensed.

For a compatible player, LoopMint’s six-month and one-year viewing plans include one-year activation; two-year and three-year viewing plans include lifetime activation. The one-month plan includes no free player activation. Ask which exact app and device are covered and how the included term applies to your renewal. The app publisher’s licence rules still apply.

Use the player activation guide if the app is asking for a licence. If you changed your television or player, mention that before payment: do not assume an old device licence transfers.

What should the renewal confirmation include?

Renewing from Ireland? Ask for the renewal total in euros, including any extra simultaneous screens. If a player licence expires on a different date, keep that date alongside the viewing-plan expiry so you know which payment is due.

  • The full amount and payment currency for the selected term.
  • The account being renewed, with its new start and expiry dates.
  • The simultaneous-screen allowance and any extra-device charge.
  • The exact compatible player and any included activation term.
  • The applicable plan terms and refund policy.

The subscription cost guide explains the difference between a monthly payment and the monthly equivalent of a prepaid plan. Do not assume that an introductory offer for a new client applies to an existing-account renewal.

Can LoopMint renew an account from another provider?

This guide covers existing LoopMint viewing accounts. If you bought access elsewhere, say so before asking about renewal. A new LoopMint plan would be a separate arrangement; do not assume that another provider’s login, remaining time or app licence can be transferred.

If you are considering switching, start with the 24-hour trial checklist on your intended device and connection. Confirm the setup and terms before selecting a longer plan.
