SMART TV SETUP

How to set up IPTV on a Smart TV

Two TVs with the same brand name can have different app stores. Find your full model first, then install the player recommended for that television.

Identify your television first

Before choosing a player, find the television’s full model name and software version in its settings or on the product label. “Samsung,” “LG” or “Android TV” is useful background, but it doesn’t identify the exact television or the apps it can run.

Send the model and your viewing country to the provider. Ask for the app’s full name, its developer and whether it appears in the official store on your TV. If you already have a player, mention it; there may be no need to install another one.

Don’t buy an app licence until compatibility is clear. A licence payment won’t make an unsupported application appear in the store.

Install, sign in and test

  1. Open the television’s official app store and search for the recommended player.
  2. Check its name and developer, then install it on the TV you’ll use.
  3. Open the player and follow the sign-in method in your setup message. App activation and viewing login may be separate steps.
  4. Wait for the categories and programme guide to load.
  5. Use the remote to search, play an item, change the volume and try any subtitle or favourite controls you need.

Keep the setup message nearby so you can check a field without guessing. If you send a screenshot for help, hide passwords and private links. The Smart TV setup route gives you the shorter version of these checks.

If your TV has no suitable app

An external streaming device connected through HDMI may be an option. Before buying one, check the TV’s connections and ask which device and player are supported. Include the hardware and any app licence in your cost comparison.

There’s a different problem when the app installs but won’t load your account. Check the sign-in method first. If the menus load but a particular item fails, write down its name and the error. If nothing plays, say that instead. Support needs to know which stage is failing.

You can also use the playback and connection guide when the picture starts but keeps stopping. Change one thing at a time so you can tell whether it helped.

Keep app and viewing terms separate

With LoopMint, six-month and one-year viewing plans include one-year activation for a compatible player. Two-year and three-year plans include lifetime player activation. The one-month plan has no included free activation.

“Lifetime” refers to the player’s licence under its provider’s terms. Your viewing plan still has an expiry date. Confirm the exact app and included term in the plan comparison, then use the trial checklist to try the setup before making a longer commitment.

Not sure which app your TV needs?

Send Sales & Setup the full TV model and your viewing country. Those two details are enough to start checking the correct route; you don’t need to send a password.
