SMART TV SETUP

How to set up IPTV on a Smart TV

“Smart TV” covers different operating systems and app stores. The correct setup starts with the exact television model, not a generic list of apps.

Identify your television first

“I have a Samsung” or “it’s an Android TV” usually isn’t enough to check compatibility. Open your TV settings and find the full model name and software version. Send those details, along with the country where you’ll watch, and ask which player app is supported on that exact model.

If the app isn’t in your TV’s official store, don’t pay for a licence until you know what your alternatives are. App availability can vary by model and store region.

Install, sign in and test

  1. Open the TV’s official app store and search for the player your provider recommended.
  2. Check the app name, developer and model compatibility before you install it.
  3. Sign in using the method in your setup message. Take your time with the private details.
  4. Wait for the categories or programme guide to finish loading.
  5. Try a few things you’ll actually use: search, subtitles, favourites, sound and remote navigation.

Don’t share screenshots that show your login details. If you want the short version, follow the Smart TV setup route.

If your TV has no suitable app

You may still be able to use a streaming device plugged into the TV’s HDMI port. Before buying one, ask Sales & Setup whether Fire TV, Android TV or Apple TV works with your television and in your country. Check the total cost too; the device may need a separate app or licence.

If the player opens but nothing loads, check your sign-in method, refresh the guide and test the internet connection. When you contact support, tell them the TV model, player name and exact error. Keep your login details private.

Keep app and viewing terms separate

Some LoopMint plans include an app activation: one year on the six-month and one-year plans, and lifetime on the two-year and three-year plans. The one-month plan includes no activation. That term applies to a compatible app; your viewing access still ends with the plan unless you renew it. Ask us to confirm that the app works on your TV before paying.
