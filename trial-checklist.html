BEFORE YOU CHOOSE A PLAN

How to test a 24-hour internet TV trial

Use the trial on the screen and connection you normally use. Check what is actually available to you, then decide whether the setup and plan fit your routine.

Request the free trial

LoopMint offers a free 24-hour trial without payment details to request it. Availability can vary by country, device, app and plan. A trial gives you a practical way to check your own options; a promotional image or category name cannot confirm that a particular programme is included.

1. Ask the important questions before the clock starts

Tell the setup team your country, exact device model and the type of viewing you care about. Ask them to confirm which categories and features are available for your location and whether a separate player app has its own fee. Get the trial start and end time in writing, so you know how long you have to test.

  • Confirm that your device has a compatible player available in its app store.
  • Ask how many screens you can use at the same time during a paid plan.
  • Ask which viewing options and terms apply in your country.

2. Test on your normal device and connection

Install the recommended player from its official store or website. Use the television, phone or computer you would actually watch on, in its usual location. A test on a fast phone beside the router may not reflect a television in another room. If you need help, the device setup guides show the main routes.

Try the service once during a quiet period and again at the time you normally watch. Check whether navigation is comfortable with your remote, whether the guide loads, and whether the stream starts reliably.

3. Check availability and viewing quality

Search for the categories and items you care about instead of relying on a headline catalogue figure. Check the options available in your country during your own trial. Picture quality can vary with the source, your device and your connection; a 4K television does not mean every item is supplied in 4K.

  • Try more than one category and note anything missing or unavailable.
  • Check audio, subtitles, programme information and parental controls if you need them.
  • If playback pauses, compare another item and follow the buffering checklist before drawing a conclusion.

4. Test support and read the plan terms

Ask one real setup question during the trial and see whether the answer resolves it. Before paying, confirm the total upfront price, plan length, number of simultaneous connections, start and expiry dates, renewal method and how to contact support. Keep the written answer with your payment confirmation.

If a particular channel, match, film or series matters to your decision, ask for its current availability and applicable viewing terms in your country. Do not assume a named service or event is included everywhere.

Ready to decide?

Choose a paid plan only when the content available to you, the device setup, the viewing experience and the total price all make sense. If any point is unclear, ask before paying.
